One word to erase from the dictionary forever: **onomatopoeia** — the word for words that sound like what they mean: buzz, hiss, pop, sizzle. Delete it and the language loses the one word that names its own sound effects, while the word itself is the most unpronounceable sound effect of all: a twelve-letter tongue-twister that sounds like a magic spell and describes exactly what it is doing. The joke lands the moment the word is gone, comes from the word itself — its sound, its self-referential meaning, the gap it would leave — and it is fresh: the field will reach for "moist", "panties" or "literally" first.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It validates the word against the bounty gate (1–77 UTF-8 characters, exactly one word, no whitespace, no safe filler) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, reading the file back to prove the round trip.
